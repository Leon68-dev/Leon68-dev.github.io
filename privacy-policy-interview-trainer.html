Privacy Policy for Applications by SoftDevelopUA

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile applications (including the "Interview Trainer" series and other software products, collectively referred to as "Applications") created and published by SoftDevelopUA.

1. What information do the Applications obtain and how is it used?

The Applications do not require user account registration and do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information collected automatically (Third-party services)

To provide advertisements, process payments, and improve stability, the Applications may use third-party services that collect data to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to display ads. AdMob may collect and use your device’s Advertising ID, IP address, and technical data about your device to show relevant ads.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics): Used to monitor application performance, crashes, and basic analytics. It collects device identifiers, crash logs, and anonymized usage statistics.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads" or full version unlocks). All payment transactions are handled securely by Google Play. We do not store or access your credit card or financial data.

3. Data Collected by Third Parties

  • Device type, model, and operating system version.
  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, Installation UUID).
  • Interaction data (app usage metrics, ad interactions).
  • Crash reports and stack traces for debugging purposes.

4. Precise Real-Time Location Information

The Applications do not collect or request precise GPS or real-time location information.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Applications are intended for a general audience (students, software engineers, and job seekers). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Third Parties and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Data is shared exclusively with the aforementioned service providers (Google) for analytics, stability, and advertising functionality.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: softdevelop.ua@gmail.com.

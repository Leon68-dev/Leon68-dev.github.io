Privacy Policy for Quiz Applications

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of mobile software applications developed by SoftDevelopUA. This policy applies to the following mobile applications (collectively referred to as the "Applications"):

Covered Applications:

  • Ukrainian Quiz
  • Capital Guess Quiz

1. What Information Do the Applications Collect?

The Applications do not require user account registration, login credentials, or personal contact details. We do not collect personal identifying information such as your name, physical address, email address, or phone number.

2. Information Collected Automatically (Third-Party Services)

To provide free content, display advertisements, process optional in-app purchases, and monitor application stability, the Applications integrate third-party SDKs that may automatically collect certain device data. These services include:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may process your device’s Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and basic device metrics to deliver advertisements and prevent fraud.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics): Used to monitor stability, diagnose app crashes, and understand aggregate usage trends to improve user experience.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to securely handle optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads"). All payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not have access to or store your credit card or banking information.

3. Types of Technical Data Collected

The automated data collected by these third-party SDKs may include:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Anonymous device identifiers (Google Advertising ID, unique installation UUID).
  • App usage statistics (feature interactions, ad views/clicks).
  • Crash diagnostics, stack traces, and system performance metrics.

4. Precise Real-Time Location

The Applications do not request, access, or track precise real-time GPS location data from your mobile device.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Applications are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). Third-party advertising services are configured to adhere to Google Play’s Families Policy and applicable privacy laws.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Any data accessed by the third-party SDKs is processed strictly for the operational functionality of the Applications (advertising, performance diagnostics, and payment handling) in an aggregated and pseudonymous manner.

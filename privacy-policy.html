Privacy Policy for SoftDevelopUA Applications

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of mobile software applications and games developed by SoftDevelopUA (collectively referred to as the "Applications" or "Application").

1. What Information Do the Applications Collect?

The Applications do not require user account registration, profile creation, or the submission of personal contact information. We do not collect personal identifying data such as your name, residential address, email address, or phone number.

2. Information Collected Automatically (Third-Party Services)

To deliver free educational content, display advertisements, process optional in-app purchases, and ensure application stability, the Applications integrate trusted third-party SDKs that may automatically collect certain device-level information. These services include:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to deliver in-app advertisements. AdMob may process your device’s Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and technical device parameters to serve relevant ads and monitor ad impressions.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics): Used to track app stability, capture crash traces, and evaluate aggregate performance metrics to resolve bugs and enhance the user experience.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to handle optional in-app transactions (such as "Remove Ads"). All billing transactions are processed securely through Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card numbers or financial credentials.

3. Types of Technical Data Collected

The automated data collected by integrated services may include:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Pseudonymous device identifiers (Google Advertising ID, random installation UUID).
  • Application usage statistics (session durations, user interactions, ad view metrics).
  • System diagnostic reports, crash logs, and stack traces.

4. Precise Real-Time Location

The Applications do not request or collect precise real-time GPS location data from your mobile device.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Applications are developed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age limit under local laws). Integrated advertising services are configured to adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines where applicable.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. Information collected automatically is processed strictly by the aforementioned service providers to maintain app functionality, serve ads, and process transactions in an aggregated, pseudonymized manner.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email at: softdevelop.ua@gmail.com.
